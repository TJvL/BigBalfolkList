# Dances

The source of truth for this repository. `dances.json` is generated from this file; edit here.

## How to read and edit this file

- `##` is a region or tradition. Every dance sits under exactly one.
- `###` is a family or a suite of dances, where one exists.
- `-` is a dance. Spellings and alternative names are separated by `/` and are **equals**: none of
  them is the correct one, they are simply the names this dance goes by.
- `{braces}` hold tags. Anything that is not "where is it from" is a tag rather than a heading, so a
  dance can be in a family, have a formation, and belong to a tradition without being forced to pick.
- A `###` tagged `{suite}` means its dances are performed as a sequence, not merely grouped.

Tags currently in use: `couple`, `mixer`, `choreographed`, `suite`, and family tags such as
`gavotte`, `bourree`, `avant-deux`, `rondeau`, `waltz`, `saut`, `aeroplane`.

Formation tags are only present where they have actually been established. An untagged dance means
nobody has filled it in yet, not that it has no formation.
